# 군중 음향 제작 지시 v2 — 사람 원음 우선

이전 합성 시안과 상행/하행 금속성 2음은 사용자 피드백으로 기각됐다. 아래는 **생성 결과 보장이 아닌 제작·선별 지시**다. 현재 연결된 음향 생성 도구가 없고 추가 플러그인 설치는 거절되었다. 실제 작업은 권리가 확인된 녹음 소스 또는 동의받은 자체 녹음을 우선하며, 이 문서는 추후 사용 가능한 오디오 생성기에도 전달할 수 있다.

## 공통 지시

Produce an original, natural HUMAN CROWD recording for a cooperative game inside a large, oppressive industrial circus. It must sound like a real group of adults reacting to a risky live performance. The audience's enjoyment is indifferent and demanding, but their voices are ordinary human voices. The unease must come from context, not electronic processing.

Prioritize believable human vocal breaths, irregular timing and natural handclaps. NO synthesized vowel drone, vocoder, metallic chatter, rattling percussion, click-train applause, chattering teeth, robotic formants, pitched UI notes, musical instruments, fanfare, riser, buzzer, cinematic boom, horror stinger, monster voice, exaggerated evil laugh, pain scream, spoken narration, words or recognizable chant. Do not copy an identifiable recording or person. Avoid obvious loop repetition, phasey doubling and uniform robotic rhythm. Do not mask a bad source with heavy reverb.

First deliver a lightly reverberant or nearly dry audition of 1.5–2.0 seconds, with genuine human room character and complete onsets. Then, ONLY if the dry source sounds natural, derive a distant seating-tier mix with restrained early reflections and a short dark decay. The listener stands below and 30–60 meters from the crowd, but the gesture must remain recognizable. Keep the onset within 0.1 seconds; do not add silence or a synthetic cue to explain the meaning. Keep cheer and boo at comparable perceived loudness. Supply 2–3 naturally different takes of each, not pitch-shifted copies. Export lossless stereo WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit if available, with headroom and no clipping. A mixed-down mono version may be used later for spatial playback. Export specs unsupported by the tool are not a reason to fabricate delivery.

## 환호

Record a medium-sized adult crowd making a brief, unmistakably pleased CHEER. Open-vowel human exclamations, several overlapping breaths, and a short burst of irregular, fleshy handclaps. Start with a few voices and let adjacent people join within a fraction of a second. Slightly overeager enjoyment, not wholesome triumph, not a sports-team chant. No laughter lead, no whistles, no metal or wooden impact. Main gesture roughly 1.0–1.4 seconds, taper naturally. It should read as “the crowd approved” immediately, without music, an ascending tone or a victory jingle.

## 야유

Record the same type and distance of adult crowd making a brief unmistakable BOO of disappointment. Natural human rounded-vowel disapproval, individual breaths and slightly varied timing/pitch. Do not replace it with a choir note or synthetic low drone. Main gesture roughly 1.2–1.6 seconds. First produce voices ONLY. If approved, create an optional version with just one or two subdued real shoe stomps or palms on a wooden rail; the voice must remain the identifying sound. No alarm, metallic descending note, cartoon failure sound, threatening creature or injury. It should read as “the crowd disapproved,” not “the player took damage.”

## 선별 기준

- 무가공/약한 공간감 버전부터 듣는다. 박수가 짤그락/클릭열로, 야유가 전자음/합창음으로 들리면 원음을 교체한다.
- 이미지 없이 환호/야유를 구별하고, 한 번뿐 아니라 연속 재생해 어색함과 반복 피로를 확인한다.
- 자연스러움과 의미가 통과한 뒤만 잔향·거리·음량을 조정한다. 현재 생성 수단이 없으면 검증된 녹음 사용을 우선한다.
- 여러 소스를 겹쳐 크기를 키우기 전에 한 소스만으로 충분한지 확인한다. 친구 음성/위험 소리와 함께 최종 청취한다.
