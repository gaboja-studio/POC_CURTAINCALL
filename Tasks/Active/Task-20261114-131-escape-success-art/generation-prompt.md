## 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept / lighting-weather paintover.
Create one polished 16:9 production concept painting for the existing Curtain Call SUCCESS ending, based directly on the supplied actual Unity screenshots. This is a proposed art development of the existing shot, not a new scene or new character design.
INPUTS: Image 1 (two-player meadow) is the primary edit/composition/character identity source: yellow rabbit at front right and green bear behind-left, both running toward the camera, full body. Image 2 (daytime results screen) establishes that this ending can occur in DAYLIGHT and that the left 43% is reserved for the existing results panel. Do not render any UI, text or icons in the concept painting.
LOCK: retain the exact recognizable existing game character silhouettes, animal faces, facial patches, ear shapes, small green bear hat, yellow rabbit ruff and harlequin trousers, green bear costume, adult human-performer-like body proportions and original costume colors. Do not substitute generic stuffed animals, realistic human heads, new costumes or new survivors. Keep the runners together in the RIGHT HALF at approximately the same full-body scale as the screenshot, with their feet visibly contacting the path. Fixed low frontal tracking shot; they run toward us away from the circus.
LIGHTING/DEPTH, MOST IMPORTANT: unmistakably overcast DAYLIGHT illuminates the near ground and readable front/side planes of the characters with a pale cold diffuse light. A desaturated pale gray daytime sky is visible in the upper part; no night sky, stars, moon, sunset, golden sunrise or sunbeams. Behind the characters, the world becomes progressively and unnaturally DARKER with distance: midground reeds lose saturation and brightness, the remote field and receding circus sink into a dense charcoal/slate atmospheric shadow bank. Make this a believable depth progression, not a black backdrop or screen vignette and NOT standard bright white distance fog. The near path has daylight detail but the distant horizon feels swallowed and unreadable.
CIRCUS: develop the same striped peaked circus tent already in the screenshot into a substantial, weather-beaten old circus exterior behind the runners. Preserve its tent identity and striped red/cream canvas silhouette; add convincing sagging canvas panels, repaired seams, damp grime at its base, central mast, supporting guy ropes and stakes, faded narrow pennants, and a dark entrance. It has real volume, weight and grounded construction. It remains subordinate to the survivors and recedes into the dark depth, with no new haunted castle, town, monsters, flames or amusement rides.
GROUND AND VEGETATION: broad lonely uneven earth path through a vast sparse abandoned reed field; dry cracked compacted soil, shallow old damp patches, embedded small stones, broken reed stalks, flattened grass and traces of footsteps. Varied thin grasses and natural reeds with fine seed heads, bent dry blades and irregular sparse clumps, never the source's triangle-shaped leaves or flat solid ground. Near details are tactile, middle distance thins out into empty ground, distant field darkens. This is desolate and exposed, not a lush meadow or a busy forest.
COSTUME TEXTURE: keep the source shapes and palettes, add credible woven cloth/felt, stitched seams, worn leather boots, dirt in creases and dried brown-red blood stains on faces, gloves, ruffs and clothing. Uneven matte dried residue, abrasion and subtle surface relief catch the daylight; not glossy plastic, clean plush or random camouflage. No fresh gore, open anatomy, extra missing limbs or new injuries. Light and shade should describe real volume while preserving the game's stylized geometry.
MOOD: escaped, yet profoundly empty and ominous; weary survival without celebration, the open world offers no visible sanctuary. Quiet negative space over the left 43% for future results UI. Cinematic art-direction paintover with grounded PBR material realism, restrained desaturated earth/slate environmental palette and preserved yellow/green character accents. Not a photoreal humans redesign. No captions, watermark, UI, score, decorative borders or comparison panels.
